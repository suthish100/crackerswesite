# Cancellation Policy

## 1. Pre-Dispatch Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge if the request is received **prior to factory boxing and transport handover**.
- Customers must notify our WhatsApp support desk (+91 89257 00923) with their Public Order ID.
- Refunds for pre-dispatch cancellations are processed to the original bank account or UPI ID within 3 to 5 business days.

## 2. Post-Dispatch Non-Cancellation
- Once the lorry receipt (LR) is booked and goods are boarded onto the regional road transport vehicle, the shipment cannot be intercepted, recalled, or cancelled.
- If a customer fails or refuses to collect the parcel from the transport godown, freight demurrage penalties and non-delivery charges will apply.
