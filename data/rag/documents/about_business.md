# About Sivakasi Crackers

## Company Overview
Sivakasi Crackers is a premier fireworks and cracker retailer operating directly from Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu—the fireworks capital of India. We offer direct-from-factory pricing with discounts up to 80% off maximum retail prices (MRP) on authentic, certified green crackers.

## Licensing and Green Cracker Certification
- All our fireworks are 100% compliant with the Supreme Court of India directives and Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization (PESO) regulations.
- We exclusively manufacture and stock CSIR-NEERI certified Green Crackers that produce 30-35% less particulate matter emissions and are free from hazardous barium compounds.
- Every cracker box includes the official green logo and authentic NEERI QR code for instant authentication.
