# Shipping and Regional Road Transport Delivery Policy

## 1. Statutory Road Transport Requirement
Under Indian Central Motor Vehicles Rules and Explosives Cargo Rules, fireworks are classified as hazardous Class 1 goods.
- Standard courier and air cargo services (e.g., Blue Dart, DTDC, India Post, Delhivery) strictly forbid transporting fireworks.
- Therefore, all shipments are fulfilled via licensed regional commercial road transport carriers (such as VRL Logistics, ARC, TAT, Rathimeena, Royal Transport, etc.).

## 2. Fulfillment Model: Nearest City Godown Pickup
- **Godown Pickup (Primary Model)**: Consignments arrive at the licensed transport operator's parcel booking office / godown nearest to your provided address.
- Customers collect their parcel upon presenting their Lorry Receipt (LR) tracking number and a valid government photo ID.
- **Local Door Delivery**: In certain metro cities and towns, local door delivery via transport auto/van can be requested directly from the destination transport hub for a nominal local handling fee.

## 3. Minimum Order Values for Factory Dispatch
- **Tamil Nadu & Pondicherry**: Minimum order value is ₹3,000 for direct transport booking.
- **Other Indian States**: Minimum order value is ₹5,000 due to interstate transport manifest mandates.
- Orders below these amounts cannot be manifested by regional road transport hubs.

## 4. Estimated Transit Durations
- **Tamil Nadu & Pondicherry**: 24 to 48 hours from dispatch.
- **South India (Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana)**: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Rest of India**: 5 to 8 business days depending on distance and highway permits.

## 5. Lorry Receipt (LR) Tracking
Once your shipment is handed over to the road transport carrier in Sivakasi, a stamped physical or digital Lorry Receipt (LR copy) is generated. We send your LR number via WhatsApp and SMS within 24 hours of booking, allowing you to track the lorry's arrival at your city depot.
