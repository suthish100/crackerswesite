# Cracker Types and Product Guide

## 1. Sparklers (Kambi Mathappu)
- Traditional hand-held festive sparklers available in 10cm, 12cm, 15cm, and 30cm lengths.
- Varieties: Gold Electric, Bright Red, Emerald Green, and Multi-Color Color-Changing Sparklers.
- Best for: Families, children (under supervision), and puja celebrations.

## 2. Flower Pots (Kottai / Pookatti)
- Cone-shaped fountains emitting cascading sparks in vibrant silver, gold, and multi-colored stars.
- Varieties: Small, Big, Special, Giant, Deluxe, Tri-Color, and 5-stage Color-Changing Flower Pots.
- Safe clearance: Minimum 5 meters radius outdoors on flat level ground.

## 3. Ground Chakkars (Tharai Chakkaram)
- Fast-spinning horizontal wheel crackers creating circular rings of brilliant sparks.
- Varieties: Chakkar Special, Chakkar Deluxe, Rolling Wheel, and Whistling Chakkars.
- Placement: Light on smooth concrete or open asphalt ground.

## 4. Aerial Shots & Sky Cakes (Vaanavedi)
- Repeating multi-shot fireworks tubes that launch into the night sky and burst into colorful peonies, palms, brocades, and crackling willow effects.
- Varieties: 12-shot, 30-shot, 60-shot, 120-shot, and 240-shot Mega Sky Cakes.
- Safety: Place upright, secure carton sides with stones or bricks, light fuse from arm's length, and maintain 15 meters clear overhead clearance away from trees and buildings.

## 5. Sound Crackers & Bombs
- Traditional high-energy festival sound crackers.
- Varieties: Hydro Bomb, King of King, Bullet Bomb, 28 Chorsa, Bijili Crackers, and 1000/5000/10000 Garland Walas.
- Strict limit: All sound crackers manufactured in compliance with 125 decibel safety thresholds at 4 meters distance.

## 6. Ready-Made Family Packages & Gift Boxes
- Pre-curated assortments designed for families and apartments with balanced varieties of sparklers, pots, chakkars, aerials, and sound crackers at bundled wholesale rates.
