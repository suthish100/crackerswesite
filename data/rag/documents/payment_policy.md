# Payment Terms & Verification Policy

## 1. Supported Payment Modes
We provide direct and secure festival payment channels:
- **Instant UPI QR & Virtual Payment Address (VPA)**: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank UPI apps.
- **Direct Bank Transfer (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS)**: Direct electronic settlement into our authorized current business bank account.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Due to road transport logistics regulations for hazardous goods, COD is **not supported** by freight carriers. Full payment is required prior to factory packaging and dispatch.

## 2. Order Confirmation Workflow
1. Customer submits order details via online checkout.
2. An official public Order Reference ID (e.g., `CR2026-XXXX`) is issued.
3. Customer completes the UPI payment or bank transfer using the Order ID as transaction reference.
4. Transaction reference screenshot or UTR number is shared with our dispatch desk on WhatsApp.
5. Our staff verifies the bank credit and transitions the order status to `Confirmed` and `Packing`.

## 3. Pricing & Discounts
- All product listings display the manufacturer Maximum Retail Price (MRP) struck out alongside the direct Sivakasi factory discounted price (up to 80% discount).
- The quoted prices are exclusive of local freight charges, which are payable at the godown upon pickup according to lorry weight parcel tariffs (typically ₹150 - ₹350 per standard carton).
