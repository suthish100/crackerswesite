# Return & Damaged Consignment Policy

## 1. Safety Regulatory Restrictions on Returns
Under the Indian Explosives Act, commercial fireworks **cannot be re-shipped or returned via ordinary parcel transit** once delivered to a retail customer. Consequently, standard e-commerce reverse pickups are not permissible under safety laws.

## 2. Damage or Shortage Claims
If any carton arrives visibly damaged or items are missing:
1. **Mandatory Unboxing Video**: You must record a continuous, unedited video while unboxing the carton, showing the outer transport seal, LR bill, and inner cracker boxes.
2. **Notification Window**: Notify our customer support desk within **24 hours** of collecting the consignment from the transport godown.
3. **Resolution**: Upon review of the unboxing evidence, we provide an immediate pro-rata monetary refund or a voucher credit for the damaged or missing boxes.
